Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Applies to the Married Life iOS app

Married Life lets two consenting adults share useful location updates with each other. Nothing is shared until each person turns sharing on for themselves, and either person can pause, unpair or delete at any time. We collect only what is needed to provide that service. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: a random account ID created automatically on your phone (no email, phone number, password or Apple ID), the first name you choose to show your partner, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Pairing: who you are paired with, invitations you create or answer, and people you block.
  • Your sharing choices: whether you share location, save or share visits, pause, receive alerts, quiet hours, and how long history is kept.
  • Location you choose to share: your last shared location with its accuracy and time; arrivals and departures at places you and your partner both approved; "about N minutes from Home" estimates if you turn that on; and visits only if you turn "Save my visits" on.
  • Places: names, positions and radius of places you or your partner propose.
  • Alerts and device: the alerts created for you and your phone's push-notification token.

Married Life does not use continuous GPS tracking, and does not collect contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers or browsing data.

How it is used

Only to run Married Life: showing your partner what you chose to share, sending the alerts you each chose, and keeping your account working. Your partner sees your shared data only while you are paired and only while your sharing is on. Lock-screen notifications never contain names, places or times.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions in the EU (Frankfurt). Data is encrypted in transit and protected by per-row access rules so that only you and your current partner can read what you share.
  • Apple provides push notification delivery and Apple Maps travel-time estimates (your phone asks Apple Maps for the driving time to Home when you use the heads-up feature).

How long we keep it

  • Location updates, arrivals, estimates and visits are deleted automatically after the period you choose: 1, 3 or 7 days (1 day by default).
  • Alerts are deleted after 30 days; unused invitations after 30 days.
  • Unpairing or blocking immediately deletes everything shared between the two of you.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently deletes your profile, history, places, alerts and devices.

Your choices and rights

Your account lives on this phone: if you delete the app, the account can no longer be used and you will need to pair again. In the app you can turn sharing on or off, pause, change what you share, delete your history, unpair, block, and delete your account. You can also contact us to ask about, correct or delete your data.

Children

Married Life is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date, and tell you in the app for significant changes.

ما نجمعه

  • الحساب: معرّف عشوائي يُنشأ تلقائياً على هاتفك (بلا بريد إلكتروني أو رقم هاتف أو كلمة مرور أو Apple ID)، والاسم الأول الذي تختار إظهاره لشريكك، وتأكيدك أن عمرك 18 عاماً أو أكثر.
  • الارتباط: الشخص المرتبط بك، والدعوات، والأشخاص المحظورون.
  • اختيارات المشاركة: مشاركة الموقع، وحفظ الزيارات أو مشاركتها، والإيقاف المؤقت، والتنبيهات، وساعات الهدوء، ومدة الاحتفاظ بالسجل.
  • الموقع الذي تختار مشاركته: آخر موقع مُشارَك مع دقته ووقته، والوصول إلى الأماكن التي وافقتما عليها ومغادرتها، وتقديرات «على بُعد نحو N دقيقة من المنزل» إذا فعّلتها، والزيارات فقط إذا فعّلت حفظها.
  • الأماكن والتنبيهات والجهاز: الأماكن المقترحة، والتنبيهات، ورمز الإشعارات لهاتفك.

مدة الاحتفاظ

تُحذف بيانات الموقع تلقائياً بعد 1 أو 3 أو 7 أيام حسب اختيارك، والتنبيهات بعد 30 يوماً. إلغاء الارتباط أو الحظر يحذف فوراً كل ما شاركتماه، وحذف الحساب يحذف كل بياناتك نهائياً.
