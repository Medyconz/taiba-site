Married Life

A little closer, with room to breathe.

Married Life lets couples share useful updates: where you last were, arrivals at places you both approve, and a heads-up when one of you is almost home. Each person chooses what to share, and either of you can pause or leave anytime. Gentle on battery — no constant GPS tracking.
