Common questions

How do we pair? One person opens Married Life → Create an invitation and sends the 8-character code. The other enters it under Have a code? and accepts.

Why don't I see my partner? Each person turns on Share my location for themselves. If your partner hasn't, or has paused, you won't see new updates.

Why are place alerts not working? Allow location access Always with Precise Location on, and make sure both of you approved the place.

How do I stop sharing? Settings → turn off Share my location, or use Pause sharing. Unpair or block removes everything shared between you.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete my history now, or Delete account to remove everything.
